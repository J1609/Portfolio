Sheet1 — DCF & 3-Statement Model Independent Case Study

DCF valuation & 3-statement financial model — Infosys Ltd.

Problem

Wanted a rigorous, investor-grade valuation exercise built from first principles rather than a template, on a company with public, auditable financials.

Approach

Built a 5-year unlevered FCFF model (FY27E–FY31E) with a CAPM-derived cost of equity, a 12.95% WACC and a Gordon-growth terminal value at a 4% terminal growth rate.

Result

Arrived at an intrinsic value of ₹952.87/share against a ₹1,155.10 market price (31-Jul-2026) — a 17.5% downside, read as fairly valued to slightly overvalued — and stress-tested it with a WACC × terminal-growth sensitivity table.

Sheet2 — FP&A, Business Performance & Strategic Analysis Independent Case Study

FP&A, business performance & strategic analysis — Gokul Agro Resources Ltd.

Problem

Needed to understand what was driving margin compression at Gokul Agro Resources, stress-test profitability against downside and upside scenarios, and turn a 5-year performance review into an actionable plan to improve profitability and cash generation.

Approach

Analysed 5 years of financial statements (FY2026A–FY2031E) end to end: trend and cost-driver analysis (cost break-up and 1%-change sensitivity), a 3-scenario PAT model (downside/base/upside), working-capital and cash-conversion-cycle tracking, and a financial health scorecard — translated into a 6-area strategic action plan with short-, medium- and long-term steps.

Result

Base case: ₹45,89,880 Lakhs revenue (13.8% CAGR), ₹1,34,140 Lakhs EBITDA (32.7% CAGR, 2.9% margin) and ₹61,744 Lakhs PAT, with the cash conversion cycle improving from 78 to 58 days. Scenario range spanned a ₹6,16,082 Lakhs loss (downside) to a ₹3,15,366 Lakhs profit (upside), and the plan quantified ₹45–90 Lakhs of EBITDA upside from a 0.5–1.0% gross margin gain and ₹10,000–15,000 Lakhs of free cash flow from working capital efficiency.

Sheet4 — Business Forecasting Model

Business forecasting & profitability model

Problem

A travel ticketing business needed a multi-year view of profitability as ticket volumes, salaries and rent all moved at once.

Approach

Built a 5-year forecast model across rail and air ticket segments, layering in margin, volume growth, salary increment and rental escalation assumptions.

Result

Modelled ₹1,31,81,900 in cumulative revenue and ₹36,75,260 in net profit over 5 years — air ticket sales carried most of the profitability even as rental costs grew 228%.
